FAQ

Axmol supports recurring community sponsorships through GitHub Sponsors and Open Collective.

To cancel a GitHub sponsorship, visit github.com/sponsors, open Your Sponsorships, find Axmol, and choose Cancel Sponsorship.

To cancel an Open Collective sponsorship, log in to your Open Collective account, go to Profile > Subscriptions, find your Axmol sponsorship, and cancel it there.

If you encounter any issues, feel free to contact us at oss@simdsoft.com with your GitHub or Open Collective account email and your current sponsorship level.

Open Collective is one of the community sponsorship channels available for Axmol, alongside GitHub Sponsors.
