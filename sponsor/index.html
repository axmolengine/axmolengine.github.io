FAQ

Axmol currently supports recurring sponsorships via OSC (Open Source Collective) and GitHub Sponsors.

To cancel a recurring GitHub sponsorship, visit github.com/sponsors, click on Your Sponsorships, locate Axmol Engine, and choose Cancel Sponsorship from the options menu.

To cancel a recurring Open Source Collective (OSC) contribution, log in to your account at opencollective.com, go to Profile > Subscriptions, find your monthly Axmol contribution, and click Cancel.

If you encounter any issues, feel free to contact us at support@simdsoft.com with your GitHub or OSC account email and your current membership level.

At present, Axmol Engine does not operate its own nonprofit entity. Instead, we rely on the Open Source Collective (OSC), a U.S.-registered 501(c)(6) nonprofit fiscal host dedicated to supporting open source projects.

By sponsoring Axmol through OSC, Corporate contributors benefit from transparent financial reporting, proper invoicing, and compliance with U.S. nonprofit standards. U.S. Tax Clarification: Contributions made via OSC are not deductible as charitable donations under IRC § 170. However, sponsorship payments are typically 100% tax-deductible as ordinary and necessary business expenses under IRC § 162(a) for eligible businesses, categorized as R&D, developer relations or community marketing costs. The formal invoices issued by OSC guarantee full accountability and are fully compliant documents for internal reimbursement, financial auditing and tax filing purposes.

This arrangement ensures accountability and makes it easier for companies to justify sponsorship expenses during audits. In GitHub Sponsors is designed primarily for individual backers and is not suitable for large Corporate payments.
